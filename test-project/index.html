FAQ

Common questions.

Do you offer hotel pickup?

Yes, pickup from your Casablanca accommodation is included for all tours. The meeting point and time are confirmed before the experience.

Are the tours private?

All tours are private and designed for your group only. You set the pace, interests, and schedule with input from your licensed guide.

Is the Hassan II Mosque ticket included?

The Premium Tour includes the entry ticket. The Skip-the-Line option also includes priority entry. Please check the tour description for full details.

Can the itinerary be customized?

Absolutely. Tours are flexible and can be adjusted to your preferences. Contact Morocco Coco Travel to discuss your ideal itinerary.
