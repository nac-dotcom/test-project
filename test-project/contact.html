FAQ

Common questions before booking.

Where is Morocco Coco Travel based?

Morocco Coco Travel is based in Casablanca, Morocco.

Do you offer private tours?

Yes. The listed tours are designed for private guided experiences, and transport or custom routes can be requested.

Is the Hassan II Mosque ticket included?

The premium mosque product includes the entry ticket. The skip-the-line product includes the skip-the-line mosque entry ticket.

Can you pick up travelers from Casablanca hotels?

For the skip-the-line Hassan II Mosque tour, pickup is available from Casablanca hotels, city center, and the Hassan II Mosque area. Other pickup requests can be confirmed directly.

How do I confirm a tour?

Contact Morocco Coco Travel by phone, WhatsApp, email, or the contact form. Tour details and pickup information are confirmed before the experience.
